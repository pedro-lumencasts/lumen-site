## 03 · The difference

**Header:** Watch time. Watch time. Watch time. It's what we do.

Nobody watches one reel and buys.

They watch. They come back. They watch some more. The more time they spend binging your
videos, the more they trust you can solve their problem.

Cutting the ums and ahs and tossing in b-roll and graphics doesn't keep them watching.

So we edit for retention. We call it Time On Brand. It builds trust one viewer at a time, and
when people watch longer, the platforms show your videos to more people.

---

## 04 · AI

**Header:** Yes, we use AI. Just not for the parts that matter.

We use AI where it works well, like motion graphics. Until it gets a lot better at edits,
every decision about what stays, what goes, and what order it goes in is made by an
experienced editor who watched your footage and understands your brand.

That matters more now than it used to. Feeds are filling up with video that was obviously
assembled by a machine, the ubiquitous AI slop. People recognize it within seconds and scroll
away. If your videos look and feel like AI came up with them, so does your expertise.

**Supervisor block (addition):** Every client has someone who owns coherence across the whole
channel — not just editors working video by video.

## Voice rules

**Never use:** unlock, elevate, transform, seamless, effortless, world-class, cutting-edge,
next level, "in today's fast-paced digital landscape". No exclamation points. No "we're
passionate about".

**Never claim:** cheapest, unlimited, fastest in the industry, AI-powered.

**Register:** one operator talking to another. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Name the
format, the number, and the outcome.
